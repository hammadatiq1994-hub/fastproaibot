General Guidance
(Informational only — not a substitute for a consultation.)

BOI Reporting: Applies to most corporations, LLCs, and partnerships registered to do business in the US, unless one of the 23 CTA exemptions applies. A beneficial owner is generally someone with substantial control or 25%+ ownership.
New Business Formation: Typically requires choosing an entity type (LLC, C-Corp, S-Corp, DBA, etc.), filing formation documents with New York State, and obtaining required local licenses/permits before operating.
Personal Tax Returns: Filers generally need W-2s, 1099s, and relevant deduction documents (mortgage interest, Form 1098-T tuition statements, brokerage statements for Schedule D, etc.) for an accurate return.
Tax Compliance: Every business collecting NY sales tax or withholding payroll taxes must file returns and deposit withheld taxes on time to avoid penalties and interest.
