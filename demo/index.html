Website AI Chatbot

Demo of the floating WordPress widget talking to the Flask backend. Open the teal button in the corner to chat, ask about services, or book an appointment.

Local test page. On WordPress, use wordpress-chat-widget.php instead. Configure API_SECRET_KEY in .env so it matches the key used here (demo only; WordPress keeps the key server-side).

Try: “What services do you offer?”, “I would like to book an appointment”, or “Check my appointment”.
